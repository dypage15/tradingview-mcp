SECTION A — SCOPE, EVIDENCE, AND WHAT “FACTUAL” MEANS HERE (Q1–Q25)
================================================================================

Q001: Can this notepad list which of my trades were “right” or “wrong” with numbers?
A001: No. Without an exported trade list or screenshots tied to bar times, only
      general mechanics and hypotheses are possible.

Q002: Is Sweep v2’s sweep definition the same as ICT “liquidity sweep”?
A002: Sweep v2 implements a precise pattern: wick through a session level and
      close back through it (see sweepIBlo/sweepIBhi etc. in code). Similar idea;
      exact bar rules may differ from informal ICT labels.

Q003: Does Sweep v2 know about order blocks or breaker blocks?
A003: Not as entry logic. Tier 3 Fibs/OB are optional display (i_showT3); they
      are not part of the default sweep entry conditions.

Q004: Where does session time come from?
A004: hour/minute in America/Chicago (CT). IB, London, Asia windows are coded
      explicitly in the script.

Q005: Is VWAP session-based?
A005: VWAP resets on the script’s new day (ta.change(time("D"))), i.e. calendar
      day boundary for the chart’s session — verify against your futures RTH
      expectations.

Q006: Can I trust “predConf” as win probability?
A006: No. Comments in code state confidence is a heuristic composite, not true
      probability.

Q007: What is the honest limitation regarding Doyle + Sweep together?
A007: Confluence between two studies must be verified visually or by exporting
      bar-aligned data. This file cannot assert correlation without your data.

Q008: Would adding “more indicators” always reduce drawdown?
A008: Not necessarily. More filters reduce sample size and can overfit past data.

Q009: Is max DD in the tester the same as the script’s $ drawdown guard?
A009: Related but not identical. The guard uses peak strategy.equity vs current;
      tester metrics use TradingView’s definitions and path dependency.

Q010: Does reactive mode enter on the same bar as the sweep?
A010: Orders use process_orders_on_close=true; logic is evaluated for the bar’s
      close context as coded — entries fire when signal conditions align on that
      evaluation model.

Q011: Can London sweeps fire outside killzone hours?
A011: London sweep long/short use isKillzone in the sweep conditions (code).

Q012: Can IB sweeps fire outside post-IB?
A012: IB sweeps require isPostIB for IB-defined sweeps (code).

Q013: Is Asia sweep gated by killzone?
A013: Yes for sweepAsLo/sweepAsHi — they include isKillzone (code).

Q014: What does “Bear pool” mean in Sweep v2?
A014: predDominant == -1 from the heuristic model comparing high vs low pool
      scores (bestHighScore vs bestLowScore).

Q015: What does i_blockReactiveLongVsBearModel do?
A015: When enabled, reactive longs are skipped if the model is Bear pool
      (predDominant == -1), while predictive longs still require Bull pool by
      construction.

Q016: Is min confluence 0 recommended in tooltips?
A016: Tooltip text in source recommends 0 so sweeps with confScore 0 still trade;
      raising min confluence filters out many valid sweep-only bars.

Q017: Does Sweep use round numbers as mandatory?
A017: No. Rounds add to confScore when price is within atr*i_confProx of a round;
      they are one of three confluence components with VWAP and Lon50 alignment.

Q018: What is the default SL/TP structure?
A018: SL = i_slMult * ATR, TP = i_tpMult * ATR from entry reference price; reactive
      uses close; predictive uses model level ± ATR multiples (code).

Q019: Is there pyramiding?
A019: strategy() sets pyramiding=0 in the export — one position direction at a
      time as defined by TradingView semantics.

Q020: Commission/slippage matter for drawdown?
A020: Yes. Fixed commission per contract and slippage=ticks are in strategy();
      tight edges flip when costs rise.

Q021: Can I infer causation from overlaying Doyle zones on Sweep signals?
A021: You can describe association on the chart; causation needs controlled
      tests (same params, hold-out periods).

Q022: What is a “common sense error” independent of indicators?
A022: Trading a backtested edge on different contract/symbol, session, or size
      without re-validation is a frequent error.

Q023: What should I log manually for each trade to make this Q&A real next time?
A023: Date, time (CT), symbol, TF, sweep source (IB/LON/ASIA), confScore, regime
      toggles on/off, whether price was inside a Doyle zone (subjective unless
      Doyle exports coordinates), and outcome.

Q024: Is this notepad financial advice?
A024: No. It is a structured review template and factual notes about local code.

Q025: Can v61.1 “Signal Fix” be summarized here?
A025: No — the Pine source for Doyle S&D v61.1 is not in this workspace.

SECTION B — SWEEP DEFINITIONS: WHAT COUNTS AS A SWEEP (Q26–Q70)
================================================================================

Q026: Define bull sweep for IB in plain English.
A026: After IB is valid, on post-IB hours, price dips below IB low and closes
      back above it, with lon50 bull filter and optional depth filter.

Q027: Define bear sweep for IB.
A027: Symmetric: spike above IB high, close back below, with lon50 bear filter
      and optional depth.

Q028: What is “depth” in Sweep v2?
A028: Optional i_sweepDepthAtr requires penetration beyond the level by ATR×mult.

Q029: If depth is 0, can shallow taps trigger?
A029: Yes — depth filter is off when i_sweepDepthAtr is 0.

Q030: Does a sweep require volume confirmation by default?
A030: No — volGate is off unless i_useVolGate is enabled.

Q031: Does a sweep require RSI confirmation by default?
A031: No — RSI gate is off unless i_useRsiGate is enabled.

Q032: Does a sweep require HTF trend by default?
A032: No — i_useHtfTrend defaults false.

Q033: Can both bullSweep and bearSweep be true same bar?
A033: They derive from different session patterns; in practice one bar might
      theoretically align with multiple session sources — the strategy combines
      OR logic for sources; review code paths for your TF.

Q034: What does swIBL track?
A034: A persistent flag that IB low was swept (used for model decay weighting).

Q035: What does model decay do?
A035: After a level’s sweep flag is true, scores for that side use
      i_modelSweptDecay multiplier (default 0.42) — weakens repeated emphasis.

Q036: Is “sweep dot” the same as an entry?
A036: No. Raw sweep markers can show without strategy entry if filters fail.

Q037: What gating sits between sweep and reactive long entry?
A037: volOK, cdOK, canEnter (includes flat time, day trade cap, DD guard), conf
      vs i_minConf, trendLongOK, atrRegimeOK, adxOK, qualityOK, reactiveLongModelOK.

Q038: What is canEnter blocked by besides signals?
A038: pastFlat (flatten window), position flat, max trades/day, ddOK.

Q039: What happens at flatten time?
A039: strategy.close_all for session flat; new entries blocked when pastFlat.

Q040: Does Sweep distinguish news events?
A040: No explicit news filter in the export.

Q041: Does Sweep use CME holidays?
A041: No special holiday calendar — session flags are clock-based CT.

Q042: Could London sweep fire when London levels are stale?
A042: lonOK becomes true after London session completes its build window; levels
      persist until reset — interpret carefully around multi-day context.

Q043: Is Asia sweep valid if Asia levels are from prior calendar segment?
A043: Levels update during isAsia; asiaOK flips when Asia segment completes per code.

Q044: Are sweeps symmetric long vs short in filters?
A044: qualityOK vs qualityOKShort and trendLongOK vs trendShortOK mirror, but
      Lon50 alignment differs for bull vs bear.

Q045: Does Sweep require close above VWAP for longs?
A045: Not required. nearVWAP only adds confluence if price is proximate to VWAP.

Q046: Can i_minConf=3 eliminate almost all trades?
A046: Often yes — many sweeps score 0–2 on confluence components.

Q047: Is killzone the same as RTH open?
A047: Killzone is a union of NY AM, SB AM, LonClose windows per code — not identical
      to full RTH.

Q048: Does Sweep mark liquidity “equal highs”?
A048: No — it uses IB/London/Asia session highs/lows, not swing EQH/EQL patterns.

Q049: Could a good-looking sweep fail because of spread spikes?
A049: Slippage is modeled as fixed ticks; real microstructure can differ.

Q050: Is entry at close always favorable for sweeps?
A050: Backtest uses bar-close processing; live fills can differ with limits/stops.

Q051: Predictive long uses limit at bestLowPx — can it not fill?
A051: Yes — unfilled limits are a real divergence from reactive backtests.

Q052: Does predictive require pool edge?
A052: If i_predMinEdge > 0, poolEdge must exceed it; default is 0 (off).

Q053: What invalidates a predictive short setup fastest?
A053: predDominant not -1, predConf below min, missing bestHighPx, or gates fail.

Q054: Is predDominant neutral common?
A054: When bestLowScore == bestHighScore, predDominant is 0 — predictive entries
      require ±1 dominance.

Q055: Does Sweep hedge?
A055: No — single net position, pyramiding 0.

Q056: Can cooldown cause missing a second valid sweep?
A056: Yes — lastSig and i_cooldown block rapid re-entries.

Q057: Is cooldown a drawdown mitigator?
A057: It can reduce trade clustering and overtrading; it also misses sequences.

Q058: Does Sweep scale size with volatility?
A058: default_qty is fixed; no volatility sizing in export.

Q059: Are stops tracked from entry or from structure?
A059: From ATR multiples relative to entry reference (close or model price).

Q060: Could ATR expansion after entry hurt stops?
A060: Stops are set at entry reference × ATR; intrabar path can hit stops as TV models.

Q061: Does Sweep trail stops?
A061: No trailing logic shown — exit via strategy.exit stop/limit parameters.

Q062: Does partial profit exist?
A062: No partial exits in the excerpt — standard bracket.

Q063: Can session flat close before TP?
A063: Yes — close_all at flatten can exit winners early.

Q064: Is that good or bad for metrics?
A064: It reduces overnight/globex risk but can cut winners — empirical.

Q065: Does Sweep filter gap opens?
A065: No dedicated gap filter.

Q066: Does Sweep use opening range beyond IB?
A066: IB is the defined opening range window for IB levels — not OR15 separately.

Q067: Can two sweeps same day from different sessions both signal?
A067: Cooldown and day trade cap may prevent; otherwise possible across sessions.

Q068: Is there a “max hold time”?
A068: Not as a timer — flatten time and exit bracket define horizon.

Q069: Does Sweep know about correlated indices?
A069: No multi-asset filter in export.

Q070: Macro event risk?
A070: Not modeled — common sense to stand down manually around major releases.
