Module 0 — Setup & mental model

30–45 min · Leave able to run GLP, draw to TV, know the three clocks

Objectives

  • Install / launch GLP, add UW API key, map QQQ→NQ (or SPY→ES).
  • Draw the action plan for the correct session.
  • Recite the three refresh clocks.

Mechanics in one paragraph

Dealers hedge options. When they are long gamma (spot above the flip), hedging sells strength and buys weakness → ranges hold. When they are short gamma (spot below the flip), hedging adds to the move → ranges expand. That is a statement about how far, not which way.

Lab

  1. Start GLP → underlying QQQ → horizon weekly → convert to NQ with Live ratio.
  2. Action Center: Bias + Session expectations + Level refresh policy.
  3. Session: RTH in the cash day; Asia / London overnight. Draw to TradingView.
  4. Confirm: Flip, Struct flip (if shown), PIN, session range band, short banner.

Three clocks

Chain / pin / walls15–30 min (default 20)Re-price yesterday’s OI
Spot vs flip~60sRegime can cross without magnets moving
Futures ratio~30sKeeps lines on the NQ/ES print

OI settles overnight. Intraday refetches are mostly re-pricing, not a new book. Same-day 0DTE that never hits OI is invisible.

Module 1 — Regime (Flip)

45 min · Classify positive / negative / transition for volatility

Objectives

  • Find Flip (near-dated zero gamma) on Action Center and the chart.
  • Classify: positive / negative / transition.
  • State invalidation: acceptance through the flip ends the pinning read.

How to read it

  • Above flip → positive gamma → suppressed travel; breakouts fail more often.
  • Below flip → negative gamma → amplified travel; do not fade blindly.
  • On the flip → lowest conviction; wait for acceptance.

Lab

  1. Note Flip and distance % in Action Center.
  2. Confirm the Flip line on the Live Dashboard / TV draw.
  3. Ask: if price accepts through Flip, what dies? (The pin/fade playbook.)
Evidence
  • Session range ~1.3–1.6× wider in negative vs positive gamma on ES/NQ/YM (research/scenario_study.py).
  • Directional lean (P(up) by regime) did not clear a proper regime-vs-regime test.
  • Playbook “RECLAIM_FLIP” as a long entry lost money (research/playbook_backtest.py).
Flip = regime boundary and invalidation — not a buy/sell magnet.

Module 2 — Pin, walls, air pocket

45 min · Label the chart without treating every line as an entry

LevelMechanismUse
PinHeaviest +GEXRange reference / magnet narrative in positive gamma
Call wall+GEX above spotCeiling candidate; weak walls — ignore for fades
Support shelf+GEX below spotFloor candidate in positive gamma
Air pocket−GEX belowAcceleration zone — not a bid

Lab

  1. Match Action Center lines to TV labels (PIN, Call wall / +GEX shelf, Air pocket).
  2. If Call wall is “weak,” say: “I will not fade that.”
  3. If pin is also the call wall → target and cap — not “fade back to itself.”
Evidence
  • Pin convergence into the close did not beat a mirror placebo on SPY/QQQ (pin_study.py).
  • Mechanical FADE_WALL / BUY_SHELF setups lost on average (playbook_backtest.py).
  • Walls still matter as map even when they are not ATMs.
Trade location and size from the map; do not auto-enter because price tagged a line.

Module 3 — Term structure

30 min · Fragile / recovering / aligned as durability, not width

  • Aligned — near and monthly agree → more durable regime story.
  • Fragile — pinning near-dated but under monthly flip → day-trade condition.
  • Recovering — stress near-dated, calmer monthly → reclaim of near flip more believable.
  • Unknown — “all” horizon or degenerate monthly → do not invent confidence.

Lab

  1. Find “Term structure” in Action Center / metrics bar.
  2. If banner says FRAGILE, write: “I will not hold this pin thesis into Asia.”
Evidence

With near-dated sign held fixed, monthly sign did not change expected session width (scenario_study.py). Monthly flip = durability context, not a second width input.

Module 4 — Sessions

45 min · Asia 18:00–03:00 · London 03:00–09:30 · RTH 09:30–16:00 ET

  • Cones are symmetric on purpose — no directional edge was measured.
  • Use wide as “how bad can travel get,” not typical as a hard cap.
  • Overnight: US options closed; book frozen; futures still hedge → width signal often cleaner.

Lab

  1. Open Session Map after futures conversion.
  2. Compare NQ negative vs positive typical points for Asia.
  3. Draw with session = Asia before the open; confirm span from now through session close.
Evidence
  • Regime widens range in Asia/London/RTH for ES, NQ, YM; not established for GC; weak for RTY.
  • Calibration: source/gex_calibration.json — rebuild with python research/scenario_study.py --emit.

Module 5 — Daily playbook

60 min · Action Center + Session Map without overtrading the lines

Pre-market / open

  1. Pull weekly book → convert to futures (live ratio).
  2. One line: Regime ____ | Term ____ | Pin ____ | Flip ____ | Session ____ typical ____ / wide ____
  3. Draw that session only.
  4. Invalidation: acceptance through Flip (positive) or reclaim/hold Flip (negative).
  5. Size stops with session wide, not yesterday’s tick noise.

During

  • Refresh chain ~20 min; watch Flip crosses live.
  • Positive gamma + chop inside band → patience, not chase.
  • Negative gamma + already “wide” early → reduce size.
  • Do not add because a wall was tagged unless your discretionary trigger agrees.

After

  • Screenshot TV + Action Center into a journal.
  • Tag: range under wide? Flip break? Invented a direction the tool didn’t give?
